## Overview

Milestone v1.3.0 establishes solid workspace mode isolation between Print Album and Social Carousel, complete end-to-end SQLite and `.afsn` archive persistence for Social Carousel projects, resilient batch multi-photo drag-and-drop eliminating macOS WebKit session clipping, and Figma-grade polygon corner radius tangent fillets.

## Milestone Summary

**Key Accomplishments:**
- Complete SQLite schema migration (`migrate_v16`) and bi-directional IPC save/load for Carousel projects.
- Native `.afsn` archive serialization with bundled photo assets and background auto-save.
- Global `activeMode` hoisting with independent viewport zooms (`printZoom` vs `carouselZoom`).
- WebKit in-bounds ghost badge preventing snapshot clipping and drag cancellations.
- Figma-grade vertex tangent fillet circular arcs ($\theta = \arccos(\hat{u} \cdot \hat{v})$) with dynamic self-intersection clamp ($d_{\max} = \min(L_{in}, L_{out})/2$) in `src/domain/shapes.ts`.
- Unlocked Inspector corner radius sliders for Hexagon, Octagon, Star, Scallop, Heart; independent tip/valley star controls.
- Compound SVG mask parser & viewBox normalizer with aspect-fit containment and Rust export engine parity.
